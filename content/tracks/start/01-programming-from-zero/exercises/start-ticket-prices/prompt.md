Time for a small project. The city zoo charges by age:

| Age | Ticket | Price |
|-----|--------|-------|
| 12 or under | Child | 6 |
| 13 to 64 | Adult | 12 |
| 65 or over | Senior | 8 |

Write a program that asks for the visitor's age, prints their ticket and its price, then prints
`Enjoy the zoo!`:

```text
How old are you? 13
Adult ticket: 12
Enjoy the zoo!
```

The other two tickets print `Child ticket: 6` and `Senior ticket: 8`. The age is a whole number. Put
the question inside `input()`, as in the sample run, so your program prints only the two lines shown.

Try your program with the ages right on the edges, 12, 13, 64 and 65: those are the easiest to get
wrong, and the tests check them.

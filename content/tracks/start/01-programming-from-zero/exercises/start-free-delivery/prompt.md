An online shop delivers for free when the basket comes to 30 or more. Write a program that asks for the
basket total, then:

- prints `Free delivery!` when the total is 30 or more,
- always prints `Thank you for your order.` at the end.

```text
Basket total: 45
Free delivery!
Thank you for your order.
```

```text
Basket total: 12
Thank you for your order.
```

In these sample runs, `45` and `12` are what the customer typed. To try your program, type a total in
the **Input for Run** box, then press **Run**.

## Design Specificity Verdict

The app is authored for this product:
- belt bands with stitches and stripes on every surface;
- the examiner's seal on drills and certificates;
- condensed rank numerals;
- the app's own marks in place of emoji;
- a forest-night dark mode;
- copy in one voice.

It drifts generic in four places:
- **Dashboard middle band:** the stat row, the "This week" panel, the cloud of skill chips.
- **Admin overview.**
- **Quest pill:** the minimised pill is rounded-full with shadow-overlay, which breaks Square Cut and Float (components/quest/quest-panel.tsx:298).
- **Sensei:** the redrawn sensei no longer looks 8-bit, but the reviewer still reads the round-headed figure as the one element that doesn't look printed.

Deterministic scan, CLI: 4 findings, down from 12. With no DESIGN.md config the scan finds 0, so all four come from the DESIGN.md-specific rules. The 2026-10-12 type-scale drift is gone.
- **1 false positive:** design-system-font on the certificate image's font URL template.
- **2 intentional:** 5.5rem (the landing's record figure) and sm:8rem (the rank numeral). Both are documented in DESIGN.md's prose but missing from its front-matter tokens.
- **1 real documentation gap:** the level-up notice also uses 5.5rem.

Overlay, on landing, dashboard, lesson, drill and evaluate. Mostly false positives or intentional:
- Monaco's internals;
- ruled rows read as cramped padding;
- the belt-cloth stripe gradient;
- the Try it code frame.

Real findings:
- **Line length** past the 65ch cap:
  - quest-offer.tsx:52;
  - evaluate-client.tsx:113 (about 148 characters on one line);
  - lessons/[id]/page.tsx:217;
  - tutor-panel.tsx:136.
- **`transition: width`** on the XP bar (xp-progress-bar.tsx:51, achievements/page.tsx:140).

## Overall Impression

The earning loop (drill, pass, seal, lesson finished, certificate) is now the strongest part of the product. The biggest remaining opportunity is the dashboard for a returning learner: when the quest is offered, there is no Continue above the fold.

## What's Working

1. A complete authored system. Belts, stripes, seals and numerals encode real state. Straw marks only position or something earned.
2. The drill loop is developer-grade:
   - per-test printed vs expected;
   - draft saved;
   - a key legend with a way out of the editor;
   - one announcement per run;
   - the solution gated until a pass or enough honest attempts.
3. Honesty as a feature:
   - per-lesson minutes;
   - an ETA to the black belt;
   - "rank can't be ground out with XP";
   - AI usage shown in tokens;
   - no invented social proof.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The quest offer takes the resume slot** (app/(app)/dashboard/page.tsx:286-352).
   - **Problem:** NextBand isn't rendered and the RankCard's action is hidden.
   - **Fix:** always render NextBand, with the quest as a slim row under it. At minimum, give the RankCard its action back.
   - **Command:** layout.
2. **[P1] The skill map contradicts progress** (lib/skill-map.ts).
   - **Problems:**
     - Warm-ups are excluded, so the map shows 0% under copy that says "solving a drill fills 40%".
     - Lower-cased tags show the topic "none".
     - The on-ramp row shows for developers.
   - **Fixes:**
     - Count warm-ups at a reduced weight, or say they don't count.
     - Keep each tag's display case.
     - Hide the on-ramp row unless it was started.
     - Hide the map until it can move.
   - **Command:** clarify, then harden.
3. **[P2] One click starts a timed, cooldown-guarded exam** (StartCheckpointButton, components/mastery/checkpoint-controls.tsx).
   - **Problem:** the clock starts at once and doesn't pause; a failed attempt blocks a retry for 30 minutes.
   - **Fix:** an inline confirmation stating the drills, minutes, pass mark and rules, with Start and Not now.
   - **Command:** harden.
4. **[P2] Terminology and count drift.**
   - **Problems:**
     - The exam is called grading, checkpoint, test out and placement.
     - Lesson counts read 108 on the landing and 168 in the app.
     - The profile shows "0/24 capstones" when the black belt needs 3.
   - **Fixes:**
     - One noun for the exam: "grading".
     - Counts scoped to the track.
     - "0 of 3 capstones for the black belt".
   - **Command:** clarify.
5. **[P2] The phone drill splits the task from the code** (exercise-client.tsx).
   - **Problem:** the expected output lives only in the Task pane.
   - **Fix:** pin a collapsible task summary above the editor in the Code pane.
   - **Command:** adapt.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Never-coded beginner:**
  - "16 kyu" in the header, unexplained;
  - a dashboard of zeros plus "133 hours / April 2027" before any win;
  - Run and Run tests side by side, with no hint of the difference;
  - "Stuck?" leads to a paid key;
  - quick-check answers look multi-select;
  - the drafted log reads "Learned: … eafp".
- **Working developer:**
  - locked lessons aren't links;
  - up to 16 sequential timed test-outs;
  - the on-ramp in the skill map;
  - the quest holds the top slot.
- **Keyboard / screen-reader user:**
  - sign-up errors aren't linked to their fields;
  - quick-check uses aria-pressed toggles instead of radios.
  - Otherwise strong.
- **Evening learner:**
  - no resume while the quest is offered;
  - a 120-minute, unpausable checkpoint with no warning;
  - no expected wait for capstone review.

## Minor Observations

- **Module page:** "By the end you can" uses jade checks on a module not yet started.
- **Drill page:**
  - the test list repeats the results;
  - the tutor's no-key box appears on every drill and can't be dismissed.
- **Dark mode:** the black belt nearly vanishes.
- **Review page:** the week-ahead bars are all full height.
- **Achievements page:** "0 of 1 lessons" should be singular.
- **Certificate page:**
  - a signed-in holder sees the sign-in and create-account links in the footer;
  - the ID, date and URL appear twice;
  - there's no "how it was graded" block for verifiers.
- **Examiner screen:**
  - the jade "Review with AI" competes with "Approve and stamp";
  - a tiny file is collapsed behind View.
- **Settings:** three jade primaries.
- **Landing on phones:** 11,100px tall.

## Questions to Consider

1. Does the strict one-module-at-a-time unlock serve mastery, or punish working developers? Should one cumulative placement grading set the starting belt instead?
2. Should the dashboard be one sentence and one button, with the record moved to the profile?
3. What should the skill map show after a first evening? Should it appear at all before it can move?
4. Should the learning log draft only the facts, and leave "Learned" and "Stuck on" for the learner to write?

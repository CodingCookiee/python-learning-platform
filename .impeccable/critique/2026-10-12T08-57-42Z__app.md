## Design Specificity Verdict

The skin is authored (belt band, rank numeral, straw here-tag, examiner's seal, "Tie on the white belt"), in both themes. The working screens fall back to category templates: the dashboard's learning-app anatomy, the drill's LeetCode split (only belt language is a "10 XP" chip), a 64-row stock-icon achievements wall with belts alphabetical, a three-card "Who it's for". Missed: no drill says which stripe it feeds; the landing's "fading stripes" for review appear nowhere in the app.

Deterministic scan: 12 advisories (7 real small type-scale drifts: seal detail 10px in 7 of 10 uses, navbar initials, quest step numbers, two quiz-check sizes, two landing labels; 3 false positives the prose documents; 2 intentional global-error inline colors that drifted, with a 4.22:1 "Try again" button). Overlay on landing/dashboard/lesson/drill/evaluate: zero contrast findings; landing's 7 hits all false positive or intentional except the Try-it nested code frame.

## Overall Impression

The drill loop's craft and the rank system are the best work. The biggest opportunity is the moments of earning (first win, finishing a lesson, first day's streak), currently undercut by contradictory numbers or left flat.

## What's Working

1. Rank as an ownable object: belt band, numeral and here-tag read as one system across landing, signup, dashboard, syllabus and profile.
2. The product proves itself before sign-up: the hero's Try it runs real Python in three steps above the belt ladder.
3. The drill loop's honesty: staged waits, results that dim rather than clear, got/expected per test, draft saved, Ctrl+Enter, a self-explaining clipboard lock.
